# Buzzer Simulator: Asset Manifest

All prefabs are original Roblox-native Part kits, generated by
`tools/gen_world_prefabs.py`. No Toolbox models, downloaded meshes, textures,
audio, scripts, or remotes are included. Roblox built-in materials/fonts are
used; there are no external asset dependencies.

## Art direction and implementation

- **Hierarchy:** CRT and phone form the readable work area. The bed is a softer
  secondary mass; quiet empty space is reserved for movement and future workers.
- **Layout:** retain the 24 x 24 stud room and all gameplay slot transforms.
  Put compact dressing along the north wall and keep the west capacity volumes
  empty. The desktop surface remains at Y = 3.35.
- **Palette/materials:** cream plaster and trim, warm wood, muted sage, navy,
  and small amber hardware/light accents. Broad closely matched floorboards
  reduce contrast noise. Built-in Fabric is limited to bedding/rug/storage.
- **Silhouettes:** raised timber bed, rounded mattress/pillows, folded blanket
  and hanging edges; an apron/leg/drawer desk; a deep tube-backed CRT with an
  inset screen, vents, power details and a separate physical keyboard.
- **Light:** a small drum pendant, matching task lamp, and a soft window fill
  use PointLights. No visible neon placement geometry or oversized yellow slab.
- **Camera:** omit the solid ceiling lid, keep perimeter cornices, and retain
  ordinary solid room walls. In-room spawn/desk sightlines and overhead orbit
  are accessible without adding any per-frame camera or cutaway gameplay code.
- **Storytelling:** original abstract sunset print, books, storage box, plant,
  notebook, mug, bedside book, and two restrained cable runs. No unlicensed art.
- **Text:** remove the floating always-on-top billboard. A small world-occluded
  BuzzerOS title sits on the CRT screen; the existing prompt supplies action text.

Design dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Rounded phone/pillow silhouettes
and repeated cream/sage wood joinery provide identity without animated clutter.

## BS-12 Worker Workstation and Trainee Rig

- **WorkerDesk1 (`assets/world/WorkerDesk1.rbxmx`):**
  - 29 BaseParts total.
  - Server-mounted when Desk 1 is purchased / active.
  - PrimaryPart is `DeskTop` anchored at (0, 0, 0) relative to `WorkerDeskSlot1` (-8, 3.2, -8).
  - Includes physical desk framing, legs, crossbar, desk mat, monitor with code lines, keyboard, mouse, and office chair.
- **Trainee1 (`assets/world/Trainee1.rbxmx`):**
  - 20 BaseParts total (HumanoidRootPart + 15 R15 limbs + Hair, EyeL, EyeR, Smile).
  - 15 Motor6D joints connecting standard R15 topology: Root, Waist, Neck, Left/Right Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, Hip, Knee, Ankle. 4 Welds for facial/hair accessories.
  - Natural seated typing posture: UpperArm and LowerArm vectors aligned with realistic elbow positioning and hand placement over the keyboard.
  - Rig parts are `Anchored = false` and `Massless = true`.
  - **Client-Only Cosmetic Lifecycle:** The rig is spawned strictly on the client by `WorkerAnimationController.luau` via `WorldService.WorkerCosmeticSync` replica markers. The server never simulates character physics or humanoid state for trainees.
  - **Reduced-Motion Support:** Evaluates user accessibility settings (`UserGameSettings.ReducedMotion`). When enabled, worker animations freeze at a static natural seated pose, eliminating per-frame motor updates while preserving visual fidelity.

## BS-13 Second Workstation and Trainee 2

- **WorkerDesk2 (`assets/world/WorkerDesk2.rbxmx`):**
  - 29 BaseParts total, related but distinct from WorkerDesk1: metal legs instead
    of a foot crossbar, navy desk mat, sage monitor bezel, amber task light, and a
    compact sage storage box.
  - Server-mounted when Desk 2 (Tier 2 + Trainee 1 prerequisites) is purchased.
  - PrimaryPart is `DeskTop` anchored at (0, 0, 0) relative to `WorkerDeskSlot2` (-8, 3.2, -2).
- **Trainee2 (`assets/world/Trainee2.rbxmx`):**
  - 20 BaseParts, identical R15 topology and part count to Trainee1; sage shirt
    and wood-tone hair keep the two trainees visually distinguishable.
  - Client-only cosmetic marker `Trainee2` under `WorkerSeat2`; no server-side
    Humanoid simulation.
- **Controller generalization (`src/client/WorkerAnimationController.luau`):**
  - Matches any `Trainee*` folder carrying the `WorkerCosmetic` attribute; clones
    a per-slot prefab from `ReplicatedStorage.WorkerPrefabs`.
  - Deterministic per-slot typing phase offset (`(slot - 1) * 2.375s`) keeps two
    trainees from typing in sync; reduced-motion still freezes every rig.
  - Records are per-marker, so removing one slot cleans only its own rig.

## Regeneration

```text
python tools/gen_world_prefabs.py
rojo build -o build/BuzzerSimulator.rbxlx
rojo build test.project.json -o build/BuzzerTests.rbxlx
```

Generation uses the Python standard library only and emits deterministic XML with
one Properties block per Item. Explicit Rojo mappings are unchanged; review
images are not imported into the game. Existing untracked preview files are not
part of this rebuild.
